# {project_name} — Findings

Results this project produced itself: measurements, computed statistics, checks run
against a source. Findings *about a paper* belong in that paper's `corpus.md` row, not
here.

Canon, not memory: mutable, never sealed, loaded on demand rather than at orientation.
`LONGTERM.md` keeps a one-line pointer to this file, never a copy of it.

Each finding is labelled **sourced** (read off a primary source), **derived** (computed
or inferred from sources), or **open** (a tentative result not yet checked). Keep every
number, condition and caveat; a finding without its scope is a different claim.
